Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) data lists the national median annual wage for dentists at just under $180,000 per year. California wages for dental specialists run higher, reflecting regional demand.

Sources used in this tool

  • O*NET OnLine (onetonline.org). A federally funded database of detailed occupation information: tasks, related jobs, wages, and work context.
  • BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook (bls.gov/ooh). The federal government's occupational reference for pay, job outlook, and work environment. Updated on a regular schedule.
  • CA EDD Labor Market Information (labormarketinfo.edd.ca.gov). California specific wage and employment projections, including regional Bay Area data.
  • State licensing boards (for example the Dental Board of California, or the California Department of Public Health). The official agencies that set and publish licensing and certification requirements.
  • CareerOneStop License Finder. A federal tool that points you to the correct state licensing board when you can't find it directly.
