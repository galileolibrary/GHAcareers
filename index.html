Sources used in this tool

  • O*NET OnLine (onetonline.org). A federally funded database of detailed occupation information: tasks, related jobs, wages, and work context.
  • BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook (bls.gov/ooh). The federal government's occupational reference for pay, job outlook, and work environment. Updated on a regular schedule.
  • CA EDD Labor Market Information (labormarketinfo.edd.ca.gov). California specific wage and employment projections, including regional Bay Area data.
  • State licensing boards (for example the Dental Board of California, or the California Department of Public Health). The official agencies that set and publish licensing and certification requirements, used for the first career characteristic.

Wage vs. Cost Calculator

Pick up to three careers and see the money story over time. It counts the years you spend in school and training with little or no pay, the debt you take on, and what you earn after. Slide the year to see where each career stands.

Net money over time

How to read this

  • Year 0 is high school graduation, about age 18. Year 20 is about age 38.
  • Net money is everything you have earned so far, minus every loan payment, minus the debt you still owe. Zero means you are right where you started.
  • Below zero means debt is bigger than what you have earned. Out of the red is the first year the line comes back to zero.
  • Every health career is already filled in. Open Adjust numbers on a card to change a value, or pick Custom to research a career that is not on the list. Any edit switches that card to Custom.

What this leaves out

  • It is a model for comparing careers, not a prediction. Real careers have raises, bonuses, and bad years.
  • Pay is before taxes, and living costs, savings, and loan forgiveness are not counted.
  • Median pay averages everyone in the career, new and experienced, so early years in real life are lower and later years higher.
  • Debt is a published average for Dentists, Physicians and Surgeons, Pharmacists, and Physical Therapists. For every other career it is an estimate: years of school times average tuition, with no housing or books. Real debt can be higher or lower, so read each card's source line.
  • Money is only one half of the picture. Your group still has to judge the day-to-day life.
